Sequence Diagram Reviewer

        

          Upload your old and new diagrams — supports .puml text files and images (.png .jpg .svg). Images are sent to the LLM for automatic extraction. The tool then compares both diagrams, highlights every change, and explains what was updated.
        

        

          ⚠ Image files require a vision-capable LLM (e.g. llava, llama3.2-vision, gpt-4o). Text-only models cannot read images.
